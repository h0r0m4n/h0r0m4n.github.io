About me

I'm a product designer passionate about building meaningful digital experiences. I combine my background in design and technology to create interfaces that are both functional and visually appealing.

My work spans from UI design to frontend development, allowing me to prototype and implement the solutions I design. I enjoy working across the entire product lifecycle to deliver solid user experiences.

I work closely with product teams to align business goals with user needs. I value research and collaborate actively with researchers and engineers to make informed design decisions.

Accessibility is a core part of my process. I focus on creating inclusive designs that are intuitive and work well for everyone.
