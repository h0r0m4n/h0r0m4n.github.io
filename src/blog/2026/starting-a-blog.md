I have been thinking about opening a small corner on this website to write more regularly about my work, ideas, and experiments. Case studies are great for documenting finished projects, but a lot of useful thinking happens in between.

## What I plan to write about

This space is meant to stay lightweight and focused. Some of the topics I want to explore here include:

- **Product design & systems thinking**: structuring complex tools, simplifying workflows, and making design decisions that scale.
- **Design systems & frontend craft**: bridging Figma, tokens, semantic HTML, CSS, and accessibility.
- **AI-assisted workflows**: practical lessons from integrating LLMs and coding tools into day-to-day design and engineering work.

> Writing is one of the fastest ways to test whether an idea actually holds together.

## Keeping it simple

Just like the rest of this site, every article is written in plain Markdown and compiled statically with Eleventy. That keeps publishing friction low—drop a new `.md` file in `src/blog/`, write, and push.

```markdown
---
title: Your Article Title
description: A short summary shown on the blog index and in the article header.
category: Design Systems
date: 2026-11-18
---
```
